Cardiovascular Pharmacology

ACE Inhibitors

Recognition: many generic names end in -pril.

Example: lisinopril — U.S. brand Zestril®; important legacy brand Prinivil®.

Mechanism: ACE inhibition → reduced angiotensin II → reduced aldosterone. Reduced bradykinin breakdown helps explain cough and angioedema.
