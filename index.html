🔬 Research & Evidence Workspace

From question to defensible evidence.

Keep literature discovery, study design, measurement, uncertainty, interpretation and reproducibility in one research workflow. This workspace is separate from Learn and Screen Lab but can send validated questions or measurements into either.

LiteratureStudy designEvidence hierarchyReproducibilityCollaboration

Research workflow

A practical starting workspace for scientists and learners.

1. Research question

Define the scientific question, population/system, exposure/intervention, comparator and outcome where relevant.

2. Evidence discovery

Organize literature, source quality, evidence hierarchy and unresolved uncertainty.

3. Study design

Compare observational, experimental and translational approaches before choosing a design.

4. Methods & measurement

Connect variables to instruments, assays, calibration, sampling and validation.

5. Analysis & uncertainty

Plan statistics, uncertainty treatment, visualization and sensitivity checks.

6. Reproducibility record

Track protocol version, data provenance, deviations, assumptions and replication status.

🛡 GPSJ Safety Center

Advance science without turning education into harmful capability.

Safety operates underneath the entire platform and becomes more restrictive as actions move from learning into connected devices, regulated workflows or high-consequence experimentation.

Safety architecture

Level 1 — Educate

Normal educational content, benign simulation, conceptual explanations and safe observation.

Level 2 — Guide

Introduce precautions, compatibility checks, consent, jurisdiction and qualified-role requirements.

Level 3 — Restrict

Elevated hazards move toward supervised testing, simulation, mitigation or benign substitutes.

Level 4 — Stop execution

Dangerous weaponization, harmful chemical/biological trajectories or other severe misuse cannot execute through GPSJ.

Audit & escalation

High-risk alerts record trigger, safeguard, review status and outcome without hiding normal educational access.

Worldwide responsibility

Universal learning remains open while regulated or high-consequence action follows jurisdiction, authorization and human oversight.
