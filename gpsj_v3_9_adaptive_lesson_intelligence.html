Pronunciation behavior

Opening speed defaults to 0.75×, but the learner can choose any supported speed from very slow to fast.

Speech reads scientific symbols by meaning. Example: √x → “square root of x” in English and “racine carrée de x” in French.

Automatic correction from facial expressions such as a frown would require explicit camera permission and a production privacy/consent design. Voice correction can be enabled when the user explicitly repeats or corrects a term.
