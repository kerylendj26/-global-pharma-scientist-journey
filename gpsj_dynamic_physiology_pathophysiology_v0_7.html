GPSJ v0.7 • Dynamic Physiology & Pathophysiology

See normal physiology. Change the system. Understand the disease.

The learner starts from the reference state, compares it with a case, follows the causal mechanism, then sees where an intervention acts. The same case expands from Foundation to Scientist depth.

Interactive teaching target: pathway nodes can later open anatomy, receptor, cellular and molecular views.

Normal reference state

Blood pressure reflects cardiac output and systemic vascular resistance. Renal sodium-water handling and vascular tone are major regulators of effective circulating volume and resistance.

MAP ≈ CO × SVR
Renin→Ang I→ACE→Ang II

Reference

Regulatory systems maintain pressure and perfusion within an appropriate physiologic range.

Case overlay: elevated blood pressure

The teaching case introduces persistent elevation of arterial pressure. The learner must identify which determinants and regulatory pathways could account for the observed state rather than memorizing a drug first.

What changed?

↑ vascular resistance+and/or ↑ effective volume→↑ arterial pressure

The engine separates measured findings from mechanistic hypotheses. A case does not automatically prove that every RAAS component is elevated.

Causal pathway

Angiotensin II acting predominantly through AT₁ receptors promotes vasoconstriction and aldosterone secretion. Aldosterone increases renal sodium reabsorption, supporting water retention and extracellular volume.

AT₁ activation→vasoconstriction→↑ SVR
aldosterone→↑ Na⁺ reabsorption→volume support

Scientific boundary

GPSJ should distinguish a general physiological mechanism from the mechanism demonstrated in a particular learner case. Disease phenotype, laboratory findings and local diagnostic criteria must be presented separately.

Choose an intervention point

Select an intervention to see target → molecular effect → physiology → therapeutic effect → major mechanistic safety consequences.

Evidence and regional validity

Universal physiology is kept separate from country-specific diagnostic thresholds, approved products, labeling, recommendations and practice standards. Those local layers require current verification before presentation as local practice.
