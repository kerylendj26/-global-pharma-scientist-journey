🔄 Adaptive progression

When a credential, degree or experience milestone is completed, GPSJ recalculates the next eligible stage instead of showing a static career checklist.

🏛 Institution intelligence

Every recommendation can carry issuer, accreditation/authority, jurisdiction, recognition range, prerequisites, official source, application timing and verification date.

Each qualification explains what it can actually unlock: legal scope, specialization, salary potential, mobility, research access, leadership or advanced study.
