Semantic speech: GPSJ reads scientific notation by meaning. For example, √x becomes “square root of x” in English and “racine carrée de x” in French; x² becomes “x squared” or “x au carré.” Country context suggests an official language pack, but users can always choose another supported language.
Example: √(x² + y²) → square root of x squared plus y squared
Audio rule: abbreviations, symbols, units and specialist terms are normalized for speech. The engine does not read punctuation marks aloud. Voice quality depends on the language voices installed on the user's device.
